I'm Stefano Scolari, a graphics researcher with a soft spot for rendering, GPU work, and anything that draws pixels faster than it probably should.

**nozbufferallowed** is where I keep the notes: experiments, half-formed ideas, and the occasional deep dive into whatever GFX rabbit hole I've fallen down. No z-buffer allowed — just the fun stuff.

## Resume

I'm an **R&D Rendering Developer at EA SEED** in Montreal, where I work on 3D Gaussian Splatting, GPU optimization, and occasional engine rendering across Halcyon, Frostbite and UE5.</br>
Before that I did my MSc thesis at [SEED](https://www.ea.com/seed) where I created [Mesh2Splat](https://github.com/electronicarts/mesh2splat), and interned at the European Space Agency and Warner Bros. Discovery.

- **Now**: R&D Rendering Developer, Electronic Arts > Montreal, 2024–present
- **Education**: BEng Engineering & Computer Science, University of Bologna, MSc Computer Science (Computer Graphics), KTH Royal Institute of Technology.
- **Working with**: C++, Slang, HLSL, GLSL, DX12, Python
- **Interests**: GPU optimization, neural rendering, inverse & differentiable rendering, climbing.
